This *extends* E1-F5: the auditor held up `multi-agent.md` as the "correct" reference, but its own top-level construction call is broken. Fix E1-7 should also add the missing `await` at `multi-agent.md:85`, and the acceptance-check should type-check the whole snippet (not only the extension call). Proof: `grep -rn createConfiguredAgentResponder docs/programmatic/{composition,multi-agent}.md docs/playbooks/multi-agent-orchestration.md` shows composition.md awaits, the other two do not; `configured-agent.ts:533` confirms `async … Promise<AgentResponder>`.
