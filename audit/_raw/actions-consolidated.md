after applying the V1–V10 verifier verdicts (refuted findings' actions dropped; severities and
freeze-blocking flags rewritten to the verdicts' authoritative rulings).

(7-consecutive-day fleet-green on the frozen build, E2-F1). Every other row is `Freeze-blocking: n`
per the verdicts, which rejected all other proposed blockers under the two-key rule.

  the core of `fleet-green-check.mjs`'s daily managed-runtime attestation. Action moot.

  (optimistic-concurrency CAS token in `local-configuration.ts`). Action moot.

Severity legend uses the verifier-corrected severity (P0 highest). "Source" prefixes disambiguate the
per-artifact IDs (several artifacts independently reused `A1-1`, `A2-1`, … numbering).
